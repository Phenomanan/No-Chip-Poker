What this is

No-Chip Poker is a table companion for people playing poker together with a real deck. It tracks chips, blinds, bets and pots. It does not deal cards, hold or move real money, or pay out winnings. Chips have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. Do not use it to run or settle real-money gambling where that is not legal where you live.
