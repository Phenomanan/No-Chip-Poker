Privacy Policy

Applies to the No-Chip Poker web app and iOS app. Last updated: October 2026.

The short version

No-Chip Poker has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We collect only what is needed to run a table with your friends, and rooms delete themselves after 24 hours of inactivity. Chips in the app have no monetary value and the app is not real-money gambling.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Who else handles data

  • Render hosts the game server.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web app.
  • Apple delivers push notifications (Apple Push Notification service) and distributes the iOS app.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Decline or turn off notifications at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Report or block a player from the "..." button next to their chat message; the host can also mute or remove players.
  • Leave a room whenever you like. Rooms, and everything in them, are deleted automatically 24 hours after their last activity.
  • To ask us to remove something sooner, open an issue on our support page with the room code (do not post personal details).

Children

No-Chip Poker is not intended for children under 17 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
